# 🚀 Quick Data Persistence Guide

## ✅ Your Data DOES Persist!

The system is working correctly. Follow these simple steps:

---

## Save Your Data

### Profile
1. Go to **Profile** page
2. Fill in your information
3. Click **"💾 Save Profile"**
4. Wait for **"✅ Profile saved successfully!"**

### Settings
1. Go to **Settings** page
2. Choose your preferences
3. Click **"💾 Save Settings"**
4. Wait for **"✅ Settings saved successfully!"**

---

## Verify It Works

1. **Logout** (click 🚪 Logout)
2. **Login** again
3. **Go to Profile/Settings**
4. ✅ **Your data is there!**
